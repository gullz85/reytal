REYTAL · Email signatures

Staff email signatures

One signature for each person with a REYTAL email address. The signature itself stays in Icelandic; only this page is in English.

  1. Find your name below and click Copy signature.
  2. In Gmail, click ⚙︎ Settings › See all settings › General › Signature › Create new, and name it REYTAL.
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (⌘V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose REYTAL for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
